Related projects
----------------

- `coxmunk <https://github.com/Tristanovsk/coxmunk>`__: wave slope statistics and sunglint of the
  rough water surface (Cox-Munk and later models),
- `grstbx <https://github.com/Tristanovsk/grstbx>`__: visualization and post-processing of the GRS
  satellite products, including matchups with above-water radiometry,
- `OSOAA <https://github.com/CNES/RadiativeTransferCode-OSOAA>`__: vector radiative transfer code
  used to compute the look-up tables.
